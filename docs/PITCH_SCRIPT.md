# 2-minute pitch script: intro → demo → findings

About 300 spoken words. *[Brackets]* are what to do on screen.

## Intro (0:00–0:20)
*[Landing page, first screen: "Guardrails."]*

> One in three customers would run out of savings within six months of losing their job, and most don't see it coming.
>
> Guardrails changes that. It shows a bank customer how many months their savings would last after a shock, then switches on protections that buy them time.

*[Scroll once or twice so the 3D guardrail moves, then click into the engine.]*

## Demo (0:20–1:15)
*[Engine page. Type `20111`, press Go.]*

> This is a real customer from the data. Today, their savings last 7.8 months.

*[Click **Laid off on EI**. The sign turns red.]*

> Now they're laid off and living on EI. That drops to 3.4 months, short of the six they need.

*[Point at the plan and the guardrails.]*

> Gemini reads this customer's own transactions and writes a plan. Our engine checks every number with the judges' formula, and each step becomes a guardrail.

*[Click the loan deferral guardrail, then the cash limit. Runway rises to 6.3.]*

> Two clicks: a loan deferral request and a lower cash limit. Back to 6.3 months. The customer approves every step, and the agent can never move money.

*[Point at Agent activity.]*

> Every action the agent takes is logged here, and if they overspend, it messages their phone.

*[Click **Why this →** on the loan deferral.]*

> And every guardrail shows the finding behind it. Which brings us to the data.

## Findings (1:15–1:55)
*[Open **Our findings**. Scroll to the correlation matrix.]*

> We started with no theory and plotted everything. The first surprise: income and spending move in lockstep. Earn more, spend more.

*[Scroll to the income quarters chart.]*

> So higher earners actually survive a layoff *less* often: 92% of the lowest income quarter make it, against 30% of the highest. It's not what you earn, it's what you keep.

*[Scroll to the loan default chart.]*

> What does predict trouble? Not income. Across 615 real loans, unsteady income and a balance dip below $500 each make default about five times likelier.

*[Scroll to the four tested findings.]*

> And when income really crashes, people cut cash, not bills. Each of these findings became a guardrail.

## Close (1:55–2:00)
*[Back to the engine.]*

> Guardrails doesn't drive for you. It keeps you on the road. Pick any account and try it.

---

**Tips**
- Short on time: cut the Agent activity line and the "Why this" click first.
- Rehearse the demo clicks once. The preset shock doesn't call Gemini, so it's fast and reliable on stage.
- In the live twist, a judge picks another account. Keep using the words "runway" and "guardrails" the same way.
